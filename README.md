# Leonida Field Journal

A spoiler-free journal for GTA 6. Everything you save stays on your phone.

## Put it online (one time, free, about 10 minutes)

1. Sign in at github.com (or make a free account).
2. Create a new repository named `leonida-journal`. Set it to **Public** and tick **Add a README**.
3. In the repository, tap **Add file → Upload files**, then upload every file from this folder: `index.html`, `app.js`, `sw.js`, `manifest.json` and the three `icon-*.png` files. Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch", choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then tap **Save**.
5. After a minute, your app is at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/leonida-journal/`.

The code is public, but your journal isn't. Entries and photos live only in Chrome on your phone.

## Install on the Pixel

1. Open that address in **Chrome** on the Pixel.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install**.
3. Open the journal from its home-screen icon from then on.

## Photos from the PS5

1. PS5: **Settings → Captures and Broadcasts → Captures → Auto-upload** and turn it on.
2. On the phone, open the **PlayStation App → Game Library → Captures**, open a screenshot and tap **Share**.
3. Choose **Leonida**. The journal opens with the photo attached; add a caption and a place, then save.

If Leonida doesn't appear in the share list, open the app once from its icon, then try again. You can also download the capture and add it with **＋ → Photo**.

## Backups

Open the gear icon on the home screen, then choose **Send to Drive…** every so often. If you clear Chrome's data or change phones, use **Restore from a backup file**.

## Map

The map starts dark, and every pin uncovers a patch around it. Once a reliable full map of Leonida is available, add the image under **Settings → Map image**, and your uncovered patches will show the real map.
